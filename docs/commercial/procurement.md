# Enterprise procurement readiness (v3.2)

The artifacts a regulated enterprise buyer needs beyond the security questionnaire. Drafted for
legal and commercial review, which is the open leg.

## Audit rights and third-party risk

- Contractual audit rights: the customer may audit the controls relevant to their data, exercised
  through the trust portal artifacts and, where contracted, a scoped on-site or remote assessment.
- DORA ICT third-party register: for financial-entity customers in scope of DORA, ACP provides the
  register fields (service description, criticality, sub-processors, exit strategy) so the customer
  can enter ACP into their register without chasing information.
- Pooled and joint audits: supported for customers who audit shared providers together, to avoid
  each customer running a separate assessment.

## Government access and transparency

A transparency-reporting position is stated: how ACP handles a government or law-enforcement request
for customer data, including notice to the customer where legally permitted, and the fact that raw
arguments are minimised and often only held as hashes.

## Export control

An export-control classification (EAR / EU dual-use) is completed for the product, given it contains
cryptography, so a customer knows the classification before deploying across borders.

## Status

The register fields and classifications are drafted here; the binding versions are completed with
legal review per the customer's jurisdiction.
